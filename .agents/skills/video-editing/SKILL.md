# Video editing (Jarvis-maintained)
Read source media metadata with ffprobe. Create timecoded EDL and pacing map. Preserve originals. Assemble clean dialogue and visual story, normalize audio only after listening/measurement, use intentional cuts and transitions, add B-roll and motion graphics when justified. For long-form prioritize coherence and chapters; for shorts prioritize clear hook and retention without inventing statements. Use FFmpeg for cuts, crops, audio and final MP4. For elaborate motion graphics, use vetted Remotion workflows when dependencies are installed. Validate rendered duration, dimensions, audio, subtitle spelling, safe areas, continuity, rights and legibility. Never say rendered unless output exists.
