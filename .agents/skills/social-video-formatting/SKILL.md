# Social video formatting (Jarvis-maintained)
Prefer 1080x1920 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts unless brief differs. Reframe thoughtfully; never crop away essential subject. Keep critical captions and CTAs away from platform UI overlays; preview on mobile. Use readable high-contrast captions and restrained emphasis. Use hook and pattern interrupts based on narrative, not fixed-second formulas. Check audio, first and last frame, bitrate, subtitles, rights, and final encoding. Provide editable source and revision notes where possible.
